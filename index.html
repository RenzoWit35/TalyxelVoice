Ctrl+Alt+Z pastes what you actually said. Select text and press Ctrl+Alt+P to polish it, or hold Ctrl+Alt+Space and say what to do with it.

      Cleanup & summary

      What happens to your words between whisper and the text field.

      Edit selected text

      Select text in any app, then press a hotkey. The text model rewrites it in place; the original is in the history, and the undo hotkey pastes it back. Up to 3000 words. The 1.5B model manages Polish and List; for commands, use the 3B model or a server.

      Per application

      Use a different cleanup in some apps: Format in Outlook, As spoken in a terminal. Matched on the program's file name, which the history shows for every dictation.

        Text model

        Formats and summarizes. Your own words never leave this machine unless you pick a server.

        Runs only while needed and frees its memory after 5 minutes of quiet. Adds a few seconds per dictation on a laptop CPU.

          Any OpenAI-compatible /v1/chat/completions endpoint: llama.cpp llama-server, Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM. A bigger model there formats much better and faster.

          Language & words

          Tell whisper what you speak and which words it keeps getting wrong.

          Say "comma", "period", "question mark", "new line" or "new paragraph" (also in Dutch, German and French: "komma", "punt", "vraagteken", "nieuwe regel", "nieuwe alinea") and the punctuation goes in instead of the word. Off by default because "punt" and "period" are also ordinary words.

          Snippets

          Say a short phrase, get a longer text. Say it on its own ("my signature") or at the end of a sentence ("thanks for the update, my signature"). A snippet skips the cleanup and formatting steps.
